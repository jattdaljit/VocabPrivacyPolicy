# Privacy Policy — Math Monsters: Kids Math Game

**Effective date:** 23 November 2026  
**Developer:** DM Arts (Daljit Singh) — daljitsingh1101@gmail.com  
**App:** Math Monsters: Kids Math Game (Android, package `com.dmarts.mathmonsters`)

Math Monsters is a math game made for children aged 5 to 10. We built it so that children can play without giving us any personal information.

## What we collect

**We do not collect any personal information.** The game has no sign-up, no accounts, no chat, no camera or microphone use, and no location access. We never ask a child for a name, email, photo or any other personal detail.

The game saves your child's progress (levels, stars, chosen monster, sound settings) **only on the device**, in the app's private storage. This data never leaves the device and is removed when the app is uninstalled.

## Advertising

The game is free and shows an optional rewarded video ad **only when the child taps the hint button and chooses "Watch"**. A hint is still given if no ad is available. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play.

Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), a Google Play Families-certified ad network. Every ad request is marked as **child-directed** (in line with COPPA and Google Play's Families Policy) and limited to **G-rated** content. This means:

- No personalised or interest-based ads and no remarketing.
- The app does **not** access the Android Advertising ID.
- Google may process limited technical information needed to show an ad, measure it and prevent fraud (for example IP address, which is used for approximate location, device type and app diagnostics). Google handles this under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6223431

## Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any personal information, because we do not collect any. We have no access to the technical information Google processes to deliver ads.

## Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K and Google Play's Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe any personal information about your child has reached us, email us and we will delete it promptly.

## Parents' choices

- You can stop all data processing by uninstalling the app.
- You can reset your child's progress from the game's Parents area or by clearing the app's storage in Android settings.
- To turn off ads on a device, keep the device offline; the game is fully playable without the internet.

## Security

Progress is stored in the app's private storage on the device. Communication with Google's ad servers is encrypted in transit.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
